# Community Content Policy — Northwind Media

## 1. Conduct

1.1 Personal attacks on other users, authors, or any named individual are removed.

1.2 Profanity is removed in news comments; in forums it is flagged for moderator review instead.

## 2. Privacy

2.1 Content exposing anyone's personal information — addresses, phone numbers, emails, account numbers — is removed.

## 3. Spam and promotion

3.1 Primarily promotional content is removed.

3.2 Links posted by new accounts are flagged for review before publication.

## 4. Reviews

4.1 A product review showing signs of an undisclosed paid or incentivized origin is flagged for review.
