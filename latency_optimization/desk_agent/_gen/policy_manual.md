## Section 1: Coverage Types & Definitions

### 1.1 Collision Coverage (COV-COLL)
Collision coverage indemnifies the insured for direct physical damage to the covered vehicle resulting from impact with another vehicle or object, or from upset (rollover). Collision applies regardless of fault. Payment is subject to the policy deductible and is capped at Actual Cash Value (ACV) of the vehicle immediately prior to loss. Collision does **not** cover mechanical failure, damage from animals, or damage from falling objects — these fall under Comprehensive.

### 1.2 Comprehensive Coverage (COV-COMP)
Comprehensive (also "Other Than Collision") covers direct physical damage from causes other than collision, including: theft, vandalism, fire, flood, hail, windstorm, falling objects, contact with a bird or animal, and glass breakage. Comprehensive is subject to its own deductible (which may differ from the collision deductible). Glass-only claims may be handled under the separate Glass endorsement (see 1.7) if elected.

### 1.3 Liability Coverage (COV-LIAB)
Liability coverage pays sums the insured becomes legally obligated to pay as damages because of bodily injury (BI) or property damage (PD) to third parties arising out of the ownership, maintenance, or use of the covered auto. Liability is written on a split-limit basis (e.g., 100/300/50 = $100k per person BI / $300k per accident BI / $50k per accident PD) or combined single limit (CSL). Liability does **not** cover damage to the insured's own vehicle or injuries to the named insured.

### 1.4 Uninsured / Underinsured Motorist (COV-UM / COV-UIM)
UM coverage compensates the insured and resident family members for bodily injury caused by an at-fault driver who carries no liability insurance, or by a hit-and-run driver. UIM applies when the at-fault driver's liability limits are insufficient to cover the insured's damages. UM/UIM limits cannot exceed the policy's own BI liability limits. UMPD (property damage) is available in select states and carries a statutory deductible of $200–$300 depending on jurisdiction.

### 1.5 Personal Injury Protection (COV-PIP)
PIP (no-fault coverage) pays first-party medical expenses, lost wages, and essential services regardless of fault, up to the elected limit. Standard PIP limits are $2,500 / $5,000 / $10,000 with state-mandated minimums in no-fault jurisdictions (e.g., FL $10,000, MI unlimited medical under legacy policies, NY $50,000). PIP wage loss is capped at 80% of gross weekly wages, subject to a per-week maximum of $500 unless higher limits are endorsed.

### 1.6 Rental Reimbursement (COV-RENT)
Rental reimbursement pays for a substitute vehicle while the covered auto is being repaired for a covered loss. Standard limits are $30/day, $900 aggregate; enhanced limits of $50/day, $1,500 aggregate are available by endorsement. Rental begins the date the vehicle is delivered to the repair facility (or date of theft report for total theft) and ends the earlier of: repair completion, ACV settlement, or exhaustion of the aggregate limit.

### 1.7 Full Glass Coverage (COV-GLASS)
The Full Glass endorsement waives the comprehensive deductible for glass-only damage (windshield, side windows, rear glass, sunroof). Repair (chip fill) is preferred over replacement when damage is smaller than a dollar bill and outside the driver's primary sight line. OEM glass is authorized only when the vehicle is under 2 model years old or when ADAS recalibration requires OEM per manufacturer spec.

## Section 2: Claim Intake & Documentation Requirements

### 2.1 Universal Intake Requirements (All Claim Types)
Every First Notice of Loss (FNOL) must capture:
- Policy number and named insured verification
- Date, time, and location of loss
- Description of loss facts in the insured's own words (recorded statement preferred for losses >$5,000)
- Police report number if law enforcement responded
- Names, contact information, and insurance information for all involved parties and witnesses
- Photos of damage (minimum 4 angles for vehicle damage; scene photos if available)
- Confirmation that coverage was in force on the date of loss (system-verified)

### 2.2 Collision & Comprehensive — Additional Documentation
- Repair estimate from a licensed facility (Acme Direct Repair Program shop preferred) or independent appraisal
- Vehicle title or registration confirming ownership/insurable interest
- Lienholder information if financed
- Odometer reading and photo of odometer
- For theft: police report (mandatory), all keys/fobs, and completed Affidavit of Vehicle Theft (form CLM-118)
- For vandalism: police report if damage exceeds $1,000
- For hail/weather: NOAA storm verification for the loss date and ZIP code

### 2.3 Liability (Third-Party) — Additional Documentation
- Claimant's demand letter or itemized damages
- Claimant vehicle repair estimate and photos
- For BI: medical records, bills, and wage-loss documentation with signed HIPAA authorization (form CLM-204)
- Recorded statements from insured driver, claimant, and independent witnesses
- Scene diagram and police report
- ISO ClaimSearch run on all claimant parties

### 2.4 UM/UIM — Additional Documentation
- Proof of the tortfeasor's uninsured or underinsured status (DMV FR letter, carrier denial, or declarations page showing limits)
- For hit-and-run: police report filed within 24 hours of loss (mandatory — no exceptions)
- Physical contact evidence for hit-and-run UMPD (where required by state)
- All BI documentation per Section 2.3

### 2.5 PIP — Additional Documentation
- Completed Application for Benefits (form CLM-PIP-01) within 30 days of loss
- Attending physician's report and treatment plan
- Itemized medical bills with CPT/ICD-10 coding
- Employer wage verification (form CLM-PIP-02) for lost-wage claims
- Independent Medical Examination (IME) may be required after $5,000 in billed medical or 90 days of treatment

### 2.6 Rental & Glass — Additional Documentation
- Rental: rental agreement and final invoice; proof of covered primary loss
- Glass: photo of damage, VIN photo, invoice from glass vendor showing NAGS part numbers

### 2.7 Recorded Statement Standards
Recorded statements are taken by the assigned adjuster, not intake staff, and must follow the approved question outline (CLM-RS-OUTLINE-v6). Begin every recording with date, time, parties present, and confirmation that the party consents to recording. Do not ask leading questions on liability facts. For any loss with SIU indicators (Section 5) or estimated indemnity >$25,000, statements must be taken from the insured driver, all claimant drivers, and at least one independent witness where available. Transcripts are ordered when the file is in litigation, referred to SIU, or the statement contains a material admission. If a party refuses to give a recorded statement, document the refusal verbatim, send the Cooperation Clause reminder letter (CORR-COOP-02), and diary 10 days; a second refusal on a first-party claim supports reservation of rights under the Duties After Loss condition.

### 2.8 Proof of Loss
A sworn Proof of Loss (form CLM-POL-9) is required on: all first-party claims >$10,000, all theft claims regardless of amount, and any claim where SIU has been consulted. The insured has 60 days from written request to return the executed form. Failure to return a demanded Proof of Loss is a defense to payment but does **not** support outright denial without a second written demand and Claims Legal review.

## Section 3: Payout Calculation Rules

### 3.1 Deductible Application
The deductible is subtracted from the gross covered loss **before** payment. Deductibles apply per occurrence, not per vehicle part. If a single occurrence damages two covered vehicles on the same policy, one deductible applies per vehicle. Deductible is waived when:
- Subrogation recovery is 100% and includes the deductible
- Full Glass endorsement applies to a glass-only loss
- The insured is 0% at fault and the at-fault carrier accepts 100% liability (deductible advanced pending subro)

### 3.2 Actual Cash Value (ACV) Determination
ACV = Replacement Cost − Depreciation. For vehicles, ACV is established using the CCC ONE or Mitchell valuation platform, drawing on a minimum of three comparable vehicles within a 75-mile radius (150 miles for rural ZIPs). Condition adjustments must be supported by inspection photos. Add: prior aftermarket equipment with receipts (capped at $1,000 unless scheduled). Subtract: prior unrepaired damage at 50% of estimated repair cost.

### 3.3 Depreciation Schedules — Parts
When paying on a repair basis, betterment/depreciation is applied to the following wear items:

| Part Category | Depreciation Method | Maximum Depreciation |
|---|---|---|
| Tires | Pro-rata by tread depth (2/32" = 100% worn) | 100% |
| Batteries | 20% per year of age | 80% |
| Exhaust systems | 10% per year after year 3 | 50% |
| Paint (full panel refinish) | 5% per year after year 5 | 40% |
| Convertible tops / upholstery | 10% per year | 70% |
| Suspension wear components | 8% per year after year 4 | 50% |

No depreciation is taken on sheet metal, structural components, glass, airbags, or safety restraint systems.

### 3.4 Labor Rate Caps by Region
Labor is reimbursed at the prevailing rate for the repair market, not to exceed the following caps without supervisor override:

| Region | Body Labor | Refinish Labor | Mechanical Labor | Frame Labor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Region 1 (CA, NY, MA, WA, NJ) | $78/hr | $78/hr | $145/hr | $95/hr |
| Region 2 (TX, FL, IL, CO, AZ, GA) | $62/hr | $62/hr | $125/hr | $80/hr |
| Region 3 (all other states) | $54/hr | $54/hr | $110/hr | $72/hr |

Paint & materials are calculated using the Mitchell refinish materials formula; manual P&M entries above $450 require itemized invoice.

### 3.5 Total Loss Threshold
A vehicle is deemed a total loss when the cost to repair (including supplement reserve of 10%) plus projected rental plus salvage handling exceeds the **Total Loss Formula (TLF)** threshold:

- **Standard TLF:** Repair cost ≥ 75% of ACV → total loss
- **Statutory states:** Use state-mandated threshold where higher (e.g., FL 80%, TX 100%, OK 60%, NC 75%)
- **Obvious total:** Burned, submerged above dash, or frame kinked beyond section limits → total regardless of formula

Upon total loss determination, settlement = ACV + applicable taxes and title fees − deductible − prior damage deduction. Salvage is retained by Acme unless owner-retained salvage is elected (deduct salvage bid from settlement; issue salvage title per state law).

### 3.6 Replacement Cost vs. ACV
Replacement Cost coverage (endorsement COV-RCV, available only on vehicles ≤3 model years old) pays the cost of a new vehicle of same make/model without depreciation, capped at 110% of MSRP. RCV applies only to total loss; partial losses settle on repair-cost basis.

### 3.7 Supplement Handling
Supplements (additional damage discovered after teardown) are approved only after re-inspection or receipt of photos clearly showing hidden damage adjacent to the original impact zone. Supplements exceeding 25% of the original estimate require a desk re-review; supplements exceeding 50% trigger a mandatory field re-inspection and total-loss re-evaluation under Section 3.5. All supplements must be requested by the shop **before** the supplemental repair is performed — after-the-fact supplements for work already completed are capped at $500 absent supervisor approval. Document every supplement with a revised estimate version in the estimating platform; do not overwrite the original.

### 3.8 Subrogation & Recovery
When the insured is 50% or less at fault, open a subrogation feature at FNOL and place the adverse carrier on notice within 5 business days. Minimum economic threshold for pursuit is $500 net of deductible. Send the subrogation demand package (estimate, photos, police report, payment ledger) within 15 days of final payment. If the adverse carrier disputes liability, refer to Intercompany Arbitration (Arbitration Forums) when both carriers are signatories; file within the 1-year contractual limitation. Recovered funds are applied first to reimburse the insured's deductible pro-rata to the recovery percentage, then to indemnity. Do not release the adverse party or sign a property damage release without confirming all first-party payments (including rental and towing) are included in the demand.

### 3.9 Salvage & Towing
Towing from the scene is covered under collision/comprehensive up to $250 without endorsement; amounts above require the Towing & Labor endorsement (COV-TOW). Storage charges are covered only from date of loss to the date Acme makes the vehicle available for pickup or issues a total-loss offer plus 3 business days — advise the insured in writing (CORR-STOR-01) that storage beyond that date is their responsibility. Salvage vehicles are assigned to the contracted salvage vendor (Copart primary, IAA secondary) within 2 business days of settlement; adjusters must clear personal property release with the insured before assignment.

### 3.10 Diminished Value
First-party diminished value is **not** covered under collision or comprehensive. Third-party diminished value claims are evaluated only in states recognizing the cause of action (GA, NC, and by case law in others). Use the 17c formula as a starting point: Base Loss of Value = 10% × NADA retail; apply damage modifier (0.00–1.00) and mileage modifier (0.00–1.00).

## Section 4: Exclusions & Denial Codes

All denials must cite the applicable exclusion code and quote the controlling policy language in the denial letter. The following are the standard exclusion codes:

| Code | Exclusion | Policy Form Ref. | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **EXCL-01** | Intentional acts | PP-00-01 §A.8 | Damage intentionally caused by or at the direction of the insured. Requires SIU concurrence before denial. |
| **EXCL-02** | Racing / speed contest | PP-00-01 §A.12 | Any prearranged or organized racing, speed, demolition, or stunt activity. Track-day events are excluded even if "non-competitive." |
| **EXCL-03** | Unlicensed / excluded driver | PP-00-01 §A.3 | Operator had no valid license, a suspended license, or is a named excluded driver on the policy. Verify DMV status on date of loss. |
| **EXCL-04** | Wear, tear, mechanical breakdown | PP-00-01 §B.2 | Gradual deterioration, rust, corrosion, freezing, or mechanical/electrical failure not resulting from a covered peril. |
| **EXCL-05** | Commercial use on personal policy | PP-00-01 §A.6 | Vehicle used to carry persons or property for compensation (rideshare, delivery, courier) without commercial-use endorsement END-CU-22. |
| **EXCL-06** | DUI / controlled substance | PP-00-01 §A.9 | Operator under the influence per statutory definition. Applies to first-party physical damage only; liability may still owe to third parties per state law. |
| **EXCL-07** | No coverage in force / lapsed policy | Declarations | Premium unpaid beyond grace period, policy cancelled or expired prior to date of loss. Verify with Underwriting before issuing. A lapsed policy reinstated by payment before the date of loss is in force from the reinstatement date forward; losses occurring inside the lapse gap remain excluded. |
| **EXCL-08** | Non-permissive use | PP-00-01 §A.4 | Vehicle operated without a reasonable belief of the owner's permission. Does not apply to resident family members. |
| **EXCL-09** | Nuclear, war, government seizure | PP-00-01 §B.7 | Standard catastrophic exclusions. |
| **EXCL-10** | Custom equipment not scheduled | PP-00-01 §B.4 | Aftermarket equipment beyond $1,000 aggregate not declared on the policy. |
| **EXCL-11** | Diminution in value (first-party) | PP-00-01 §B.9 | First-party DV is not a covered loss. |
| **EXCL-12** | Misrepresentation / fraud | PP-00-01 Conditions §3 | Material misrepresentation in the application or in the presentation of a claim. Voids coverage ab initio for the claim. Requires SIU and Claims Legal sign-off. |

### 4.1 Denial Procedure
Before issuing any denial: (1) confirm the facts supporting the exclusion are documented in the file with source (statement, police report, DMV record, inspection); (2) confirm the cited policy form and edition match the insured's declarations page; (3) run the denial past the authority matrix in Section 6.2; (4) generate the denial letter from the template library — free-typed denial letters are prohibited. Denials citing EXCL-03, EXCL-06, or EXCL-08 must attach the underlying evidence (MVR printout, toxicology summary, or theft report) to the file before the letter is released. Denials must be sent by first-class mail and, where the insured has opted in, by email; certified mail is required for EXCL-07 and EXCL-12.

### 4.2 Appeals & Reconsideration
The insured or claimant may appeal any denial in writing within 60 days of the denial letter date. Appeals are logged under file activity code `APPEAL` and routed to a Senior Adjuster who did not handle the original decision. The reviewer must issue a written appeal decision within 21 days, either affirming the denial (with additional explanation addressing each point raised) or reversing and authorizing payment. A second-level appeal goes to the Claims Manager and is the final internal step; the response must include the state DOI contact information regardless of outcome.

**Partial denials:** When only a portion of the claim is excluded (e.g., mechanical failure caused a collision — collision damage covered, engine repair excluded under EXCL-04), issue a partial denial letter itemizing covered vs. excluded amounts.

## Section 5: Fraud Indicators & SIU Escalation

### 5.1 Fraud Indicator Scoring Rubric
Score each claim at FNOL and again at estimate review. Assign points per indicator present; a cumulative score of **6 or higher** requires mandatory SIU referral via form SIU-REF-1. Scores of 3–5 require supervisor review before payment.

| Indicator | Points |
|---|---|
| Loss reported >30 days after occurrence without documented reason | 2 |
| Policy incepted or coverage increased ≤30 days before loss | 3 |
| Prior claims frequency: ≥3 claims in 36 months on same policy or insured | 2 |
| Damage inconsistent with described facts (e.g., rear damage in claimed frontal collision) | 3 |
| No police report on theft, vandalism, or hit-and-run | 2 |
| Insured pushes for quick cash settlement, refuses inspection | 2 |
| Vehicle purchased ≤60 days before total-loss theft or fire | 3 |
| All damage is pre-existing per CARFAX/prior photos | 4 |
| Claimant/insured is evasive, contradictory, or coached in recorded statement | 2 |
| Medical treatment grossly disproportionate to impact severity (MIST claim) | 2 |
| Same body shop, clinic, or attorney appears in ≥3 flagged claims in 12 months | 3 |
| Receipts, title, or documents show signs of alteration | 4 |
| Insured has financial distress indicators (bankruptcy, repossession notice on file) | 1 |
| VIN inconsistencies between title, dash plate, and door jamb | 4 |

### 5.2 Mandatory SIU Referral Triggers (regardless of score)
- Any theft or fire total loss with vehicle value >$25,000
- Any claim where EXCL-01 (intentional) or EXCL-12 (fraud) denial is contemplated
- Staged-accident pattern match from ISO ClaimSearch or NICB alert
- Owner-give-up indicators: vehicle recovered stripped in <72 hours, keys in ignition, insured behind on payments
- Any claim where an involved party is on the internal Watch List

### 5.3 SIU Handling Rules
Once referred, the field adjuster **must not** issue payment, take a final recorded statement, or send a denial without SIU written concurrence. SIU has 14 calendar days to accept or return the referral. If SIU accepts, the claim is placed in status `HOLD-SIU` and regulatory-clock extensions must be requested per Section 8.

### 5.4 Examination Under Oath (EUO)
An EUO is a formal, sworn examination of the insured conducted by Claims Legal or retained counsel, authorized under the policy's Duties After Loss condition. Request an EUO when: SIU score ≥8; documentary evidence is materially inconsistent; or the loss exceeds $40,000 with any single 3-point indicator present. The EUO demand letter (CORR-EUO-01) must give at least 14 days' notice, offer at least two date options, and list the documents the insured must bring (title, finance contract, phone records, bank statements for 90 days pre-loss, all keys). An insured's unexcused failure to appear after two properly noticed EUO dates is a breach of policy conditions and supports denial — but only with Claims Legal written concurrence. EUO transcripts are privileged work product; do not share with claimants or third parties.

### 5.5 NICB & Law Enforcement Coordination
SIU is the sole point of contact with the National Insurance Crime Bureau and law enforcement. Field adjusters do not contact police detectives directly regarding fraud suspicion. When SIU confirms probable fraud, SIU files the state fraud bureau referral (mandatory in CA, FL, NJ, NY, MA within 60 days of determination) and NICB questionable-claim submission. A fraud denial under EXCL-12 may be issued before criminal resolution, but any reference to criminal proceedings in customer correspondence must be pre-approved by Claims Legal.

## Section 6: Authority Limits & Approval Matrix

### 6.1 Payment Authority
Authority is measured against **gross indemnity** on the claim (all features combined), not net of deductible.

| Role | Indemnity Authority | Reserve Authority | Expense Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adjuster I | $7,500 | $15,000 | $1,000 |
| Adjuster II | $15,000 | $30,000 | $2,500 |
| Senior Adjuster | $50,000 | $100,000 | $5,000 |
| Claims Supervisor | $100,000 | $200,000 | $10,000 |
| Claims Manager | $150,000 | $300,000 | $25,000 |
| Regional Claims Director | $500,000 | $1,000,000 | $75,000 |
| Large Loss Committee | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

### 6.2 Escalation Rules
- Any single payment exceeding role authority requires **written approval** from the next level up in ClaimCenter before check issuance.
- The Large Loss Committee (Claims VP, Claims Legal, Actuarial, Regional Director) must review any claim reserved >$500,000, any claim with extra-contractual (bad faith) exposure, and any fatality.
- Authority is **reduced by 50%** for any claim with an SIU score ≥3 until SIU clears the file.
- Coverage denials (full denial of a claim feature) require Senior Adjuster or above, plus a Coverage Opinion memo on file for denials citing EXCL-01, EXCL-06, EXCL-07, or EXCL-12.

### 6.3 Reserve Adequacy
Reserves must reflect probable ultimate exposure, not best-case. Reserves are reviewed at 30/60/90 days and at any adverse development. Stair-stepping (repeated small reserve increases to avoid authority triggers) is prohibited and audited monthly.

## Section 7: Customer Communication Templates

Use the following approved language blocks. Do not alter bracketed regulatory text. Full templates are in the Correspondence Library (CORR-LIB-24).

### 7.1 Acknowledgment of Claim (Template ACK-01)
> "We have received your claim reported on [DATE OF FNOL] regarding a loss occurring on [DATE OF LOSS]. Your claim number is [CLAIM #]. Your adjuster is [NAME], who can be reached at [PHONE/EMAIL]. We will complete our investigation and advise you of our coverage decision within the timeframe required by your state."

### 7.2 Request for Information (Template RFI-03)
> "To continue our evaluation of your claim, we need the following documents: [LIST]. Please provide these within 15 days of this letter. Failure to provide requested documentation may delay resolution and, where permitted by your policy, may affect coverage. [Insert state-specific cooperation clause language.]"

### 7.3 Payment / Approval (Template PAY-02)
> "We have completed our review and have approved payment in the amount of $[AMOUNT], representing [DESCRIPTION: e.g., cost of repair less your $500 collision deductible]. Payment has been issued to [PAYEE] on [DATE]. If you or your repair facility identify additional damage related to this loss, please contact your adjuster before repairs are performed."

### 7.4 Partial Denial (Template DEN-PART-01)
> "We have approved $[COVERED AMOUNT] for the covered portion of your loss. However, we are unable to pay $[EXCLUDED AMOUNT] for [ITEM], because [plain-language reason]. This determination is based on the following policy provision: '[QUOTE POLICY LANGUAGE]' (Exclusion [EXCL-CODE]). You have the right to appeal this decision as described below."

### 7.5 Full Denial (Template DEN-FULL-01)
> "After careful review, we must respectfully deny coverage for this claim. Our investigation determined that [factual basis]. Your policy excludes coverage for this loss under [EXCL-CODE]: '[QUOTE POLICY LANGUAGE].' [Insert state-mandated appeal rights and DOI contact paragraph — CORR-LIB-24 Appendix B.] This denial does not waive any other policy terms, conditions, or defenses."

### 7.6 Total Loss Settlement Offer (Template TL-04)
> "We have determined your vehicle to be a total loss. Based on market valuation of comparable vehicles in your area, the Actual Cash Value of your [YEAR MAKE MODEL] is $[ACV]. Adding sales tax of $[TAX] and title/registration fees of $[FEES], and subtracting your deductible of $[DED], our settlement offer is $[NET]. A copy of the valuation report is enclosed. Please sign and return the enclosed Power of Attorney and title documents."

### 7.7 Reservation of Rights (Template ROR-01)
> "We are investigating this claim under a reservation of rights. This means we have identified a question as to whether coverage applies, specifically regarding [ISSUE]. We will continue to investigate and defend as appropriate, but we reserve the right to deny coverage, withdraw from defense, or seek reimbursement if it is determined that coverage does not apply. This letter is not a denial."

## Section 8: Regulatory & Timeline Requirements

### 8.1 Unfair Claims Practices — General Standards
All handling must comply with the NAIC Unfair Claims Settlement Practices Model Act as adopted in each state. Core obligations: acknowledge claims promptly, investigate promptly and fairly, affirm or deny coverage within a reasonable time, offer fair settlement when liability is reasonably clear, and provide a written explanation for any denial citing specific policy language.

### 8.2 State Timeline Matrix
The table below reflects the strictest deadlines for high-volume states. Where the insured's state is not listed, apply the **default row**. All days are calendar days from receipt unless noted.

| State | Acknowledge FNOL | Begin Investigation | Accept/Deny Decision | Pay After Acceptance | Status Letter Interval |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CA | 15 days | 15 days | 40 days from proof of claim | 30 days | Every 30 days |
| TX | 15 days | 15 days | 15 business days from all docs | 5 business days | n/a (statutory) |
| FL | 14 days | Upon receipt | 90 days | 20 days | Every 30 days |
| NY | 15 business days | 15 business days | 15 business days from proof | 5 business days | Every 90 days |
| IL | 15 days | 30 days | 60 days | 30 days | Every 60 days |
| GA | Prompt | Prompt | 60 days from proof | 10 days | Every 60 days |
| PA | 10 days | 30 days | 15 days from proof | Prompt | Every 45 days |
| MI | Prompt | Prompt | 60 days (PIP: 30 days) | 30 days | Every 30 days |
| **Default** | 15 days | 15 days | 30 days from proof | 10 days | Every 30 days |

### 8.3 Clock Management Rules
- The "proof of claim" clock starts when **all** documentation reasonably necessary to decide the claim has been received — not at FNOL. Document each RFI and each receipt in the file notes to establish the clock.
- If unable to accept or deny within the statutory window, send a written status/delay letter citing the specific reason and the additional time needed, and diary the file for the next interval.
- SIU referral does **not** automatically toll the statutory clock. Request an extension in writing and, where required (CA, NY), notify the insured that the claim is under investigation.
- Interest penalties accrue on late payments in TX (18% annual), FL (statutory rate), NY (2%/month on PIP), and MI (12% on PIP). Late-payment interest is coded to expense, not indemnity, and is reportable on the adjuster's quality scorecard.

### 8.4 Record Retention
All claim files, including photos, estimates, correspondence, and recorded statements, are retained for 7 years after closure (10 years for BI claims involving a minor, measured from the minor's 18th birthday). Litigation-hold notices override standard retention and suspend all destruction.

### 8.5 Complaint Handling
Any written complaint from an insured, claimant, attorney, or Department of Insurance must be logged in the Complaint Register within 24 hours and escalated to the Claims Supervisor. DOI complaints require a written response within the timeframe stated in the DOI letter (typically 15–21 days) and must be reviewed by Claims Compliance before sending.
