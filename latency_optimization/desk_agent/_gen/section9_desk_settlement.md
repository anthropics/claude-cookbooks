## Section 9: Desk Settlement Procedure (Live Calls)

Use this procedure when an adjuster asks for a settlement figure on a first-party physical damage claim while the customer is on the line. Work the steps in order, using only facts in the claim file. Sections 1 to 6 supply the tables and definitions; where they leave room for interpretation, this section controls. Documentation, correspondence, sign-off and regulatory steps (Sections 2, 7 and 8, and 4.1, 4.2, 5.3 to 5.5, 6.2 and 6.3) happen after the call and never change the verdict or the amount.

### 9.1 Coverage in force
An endorsement counts only if its effective date is on or before the date of loss. This applies everywhere an endorsement matters below.
The date of loss must fall on or after policy inception. If the policy lapsed, a loss on or after the lapse date and before the reinstatement date is excluded (EXCL-07). A loss on or after the reinstatement date is covered. Not in force: verdict DENY.

### 9.2 Exclusions
Apply the Section 4 table, including the limits written into each row. In particular: EXCL-08 never applies to a family member who lives in the insured's household (same address as the named insured); it does apply to family members who live elsewhere and to people who are not family; a named excluded driver is EXCL-03 whatever their relationship or permission; EXCL-05 does not apply when END-CU-22 is on the policy. An exclusion that reaches the whole loss: verdict DENY. A denial is reported as DENY whichever role has to sign it. EXCL-04 and EXCL-10 only remove or limit individual estimate lines (9.4).

### 9.3 Which coverage responds
Classify the cause of loss under 1.1 or 1.2. A parked vehicle struck by another vehicle is Collision. If the policy does not carry the coverage that responds: verdict DENY. The deductible used in 9.6 is the deductible of the coverage that responds.

### 9.4 Adjusted repair cost
Re-price every estimate line against Appendix A; the shop's billed figures are never used as is.
1. Area: a catalogue line whose Appendix A area is not among the areas the appraiser confirmed is not loss-related and is removed in full (EXCL-04). Use the Area column exactly as printed; LH or RH in a part name does not change the area. Lines without a catalogue number (refinish, paint and materials, sublet, aftermarket) have no area and are never removed under this item.
2. Duplicates: a catalogue number is paid once per claim. Later lines with the same number are removed.
3. Part price: the lower of the billed price and the Appendix A list price.
4. Labor: the lower of the billed hours and the Appendix A book hours, x the lower of the shop's rate and the 3.4 cap for the shop's state and that labor type.
   Included operations: where a line that survives items 1 and 2 has entries in the Appendix A column "Book hours include the labor for", no labor is paid on those catalogue numbers anywhere on the estimate. Their parts are still paid. This holds for ordinary and supplement lines alike, and even when the including line's own labor is not paid.
5. Wear items (Appendix A wear category, 3.3 schedule): part age = whole years from the date the part was last replaced per the service history, or from the vehicle's first in-service date if it never was, to the date of loss. Depreciation percent = yearly rate x (age - the years stated after "after year", if any), never below 0% and never above the stated maximum. It reduces the payable part price only, not the labor.
6. Refinish labor: payable hours = the lower of the billed hours and the refinish allowance, which is 1.5 hours for each Appendix A refinish panel that survives items 1 and 2 (ordinary and supplement lines alike), plus 1.0 hour for blending, which is allowed even when no refinish panel survives. The rate cap applies. No depreciation is taken on refinish labor or on paint and materials.
   Paint and materials: the lower of the billed amount and $40 x the payable refinish hours. If the result is above $450 it is paid at $450 unless an itemized paint and materials invoice is on file.
   Sublet lines with an invoice on file are paid as billed.
7. Aftermarket (custom) equipment lines are paid up to $1,000 in total across all such lines, unless a custom equipment schedule is in effect, in which case they are paid in full (EXCL-10).
8. Supplement lines sent after the work was already finished, with no supervisor approval on file, are re-priced under items 1 to 5 and then paid up to $500 in total (3.7). Supplements requested before the work and approved are ordinary lines. The 25% and 50% review thresholds in 3.7 are handled after the call.
The adjusted repair cost is the sum of what remains.

### 9.5 Total loss test
ACV = market valuation + unscheduled aftermarket equipment with receipts (at most $1,000) - 50% of the estimated cost of prior unrepaired damage (3.2). The vehicle is a total loss when adjusted repair cost x 1.10 (the supplement reserve) is at or above the threshold percentage of ACV. The threshold is 75%, or the 3.5 figure for the state where the vehicle is garaged (the policy address) where that is higher. Leave rental and salvage handling out of the desk test.
- Total loss: vehicle amount = ACV + sales tax on the ACV at the rate shown in the file + the title fee.
- Otherwise: vehicle amount = adjusted repair cost.
Prior damage is already inside ACV; do not subtract it again.

### 9.6 Deductible
Subtract the deductible from the vehicle amount, not below zero, unless 3.1 waives it. The fault-based waiver needs both conditions: the insured is 0% at fault and the other carrier has accepted 100% liability.

### 9.7 Towing and rental
Towing per 3.9. Rental per 1.6, only if a rental endorsement is on the policy: payable days = calendar days from the later of rental pickup and delivery to the shop, to the earlier of rental return and repair completion or settlement (May 3 to May 10 is 7 days); daily rate = the lower of the billed rate and the daily limit; the result is capped at the aggregate limit. The deductible does not apply to towing or rental.

### 9.8 Amounts
Gross indemnity = vehicle amount before the deductible + payable towing + payable rental.
Amount payable = vehicle amount after the deductible + payable towing + payable rental.

### 9.9 Fraud score
Score the claim with the 5.1 table, counting only indicators the file supports.
- Day counts are calendar days between the two dates (June 1 to July 1 is 30 days).
- A window of N months ending on the date of loss runs from the same calendar day N months earlier up to the day before the date of loss.
- Only the policy inception date and "limits increased" entries in the change log count for the 30-day indicator, each counted from the date printed. A reinstatement does not.
- Prior claim frequency counts this policyholder's ledger rows with status "paid" and a loss date in the 36 months ending on this date of loss. Claims closed without payment and the current claim are not counted.
- The repair shop indicator counts ledger rows, for any policyholder, at the shop on this estimate, with SIU flag Y and a loss date in the 12 months ending on this date of loss. The full shop name must match; shops with similar names are different businesses.
- "Damage inconsistent with described facts" is scored only when the appraiser says so in the file notes, or when two or more estimate lines were removed under 9.4 item 1. Do not score it on your own view of the estimate.
- The police report indicator applies only to theft, vandalism and hit-and-run losses.
- Financial distress is scored only on what the 5.1 row names. A lapse for non-payment is not a financial distress indicator.
Also check the 5.2 mandatory triggers. Only two can be decided from a desk file: the Watch List trigger, and the theft or fire total loss trigger (any theft or fire loss that 9.5 tests as a total loss, with the 9.5 ACV above $25,000). The owner-give-up trigger applies only to a stolen vehicle that has been recovered; a repossession notice on its own is the 1-point financial distress indicator, not a trigger.

### 9.10 Verdict
Take the first row that applies. An amount payable of $0 is still APPROVE or SUPERVISOR, not DENY.

| Condition | Verdict | Amount to report |
|---|---|---|
| 9.1, 9.2 or 9.3 ends the claim | DENY | 0 |
| Score of 6 or more, or any 5.2 trigger | FRAUD (SIU referral; no payment, 5.3) | 0 |
| Score of 3 to 5 | SUPERVISOR | amount payable |
| Gross indemnity above the handling adjuster's indemnity authority (6.1) | SUPERVISOR | amount payable |
| Otherwise | APPROVE | amount payable |
